# Bible playlist: intro plus six videos

Playlist title: **Bible in MakeChurchEazy**
Playlist description: Find any verse in seconds and put it on your church livestream. Seven short videos, from your first verse to running scripture without touching the mouse.

Every video is one task, the title says what the viewer can do afterwards, and none runs longer than 3 minutes.

> **Before recording.** The button and menu names below come from the app's own text (Bible tab strings), but this was written from the code, not from clicking through the app. Open each screen while you record and use the label you actually see. This playlist covers the **Bible tab in the OBS Dock**. "Preview, then Send" belongs to Studio Mode, so it gets its own video in the Studio playlist, not here.

## House rules for all seven

- **Start with the Bible, never with themes.** Videos 0 to 4 use the app's default look and never open a theme picker or say the word "theme". A viewer should be able to get a verse on screen without knowing what a theme is. Themes first appear in video 5, which is optional, and the deeper theme work lives in the Themes playlist.
- Say "lower third" and "full screen", not "lower third theme" or "fullscreen theme". The app labels these modes with the word theme in places; skip over that.
- If a screen shows a theme name or picker you did not need, crop it, zoom past it, or start the recording after it.
- Same sample church, same speaker name, same verses in every video (John 3:16, Psalm 23, Romans 8:28 work well).
- First 5 seconds: say what the viewer will be able to do by the end.
- Record the Dock inside OBS with a visible preview, so people can see the verse arrive on the stream.
- Title card of 2 seconds, no long intro. End card points to the next video.
- Put the steps as text under each video (the "In this video" list below).

## 0. Intro: Show Bible verses on your church stream (60 to 90 sec)

**Goal:** show what the Bible tab does so the viewer knows why to keep watching.

**Show, in this order**
1. Church stream running in OBS, speaker on camera.
2. Type `j316` in the Bible tab, press Enter. The verse appears as a lower third.
3. Switch to full screen. Same verse, shown full screen.
4. Compare two translations side by side.
5. Clear it. Stream returns to the camera.

**Say:** "This is the Bible tab in MakeChurchEazy. In the next six short videos you'll learn how to find a verse, put it on your stream, switch translations, compare them, make it look like your church, and run it from the keyboard. Let's start."

**In this video:** what the Bible tab can do, and what the next six videos cover.

## 1. How to find any Bible verse fast (2 min)

**Goal:** the viewer can open any verse in under 5 seconds.

**Show**
1. Search box: type `j316` (shorthand), then `john 3:16`, then a phrase like "for God so loved the world". Point out the hint text in the box.
2. Press Enter to open the result.
3. Browse instead: choose Book, then Chapter, then Verse.
4. Recent searches and Bible History: open a verse you used earlier. Show History's search and sort (today, this week, newest first).
5. Favorites: add a verse or theme you use every week.

**Say:** "You don't need to type the whole book name. Short forms like j316 work, and so does a phrase from the verse."

**In this video:** search by shorthand, by reference, and by words; browse by book; History and recent searches.

---

## 2. How to show a verse on your stream (3 min)

**Goal:** the viewer can put a verse on the stream and take it off again.

**Show**
1. Pick a verse, press Enter (or click it) to send it to OBS. Show it arriving on the stream.
2. Overlay mode: switch between **Lower Third** and **Full Screen**. Say when to use which (lower third while the pastor speaks, full screen when the whole room reads together). Use the default look as it is: do not open Change Theme here.
3. Press Esc, or use Clear, to take it off.
4. Show the live preview in the Dock updating before and after.

**Say:** "Lower third keeps the speaker visible. Full screen is for the moment everyone reads the verse together."

**In this video:** send a verse to OBS, switch lower third and full screen, clear the screen.

---

## 3. How to change Bible translations and add more (2 min)

**Goal:** the viewer can show their church's preferred translation.

**Show**
1. Select Bible version: the list, the **Installed** label, the search box (Search versions).
2. Switch from KJV to another installed version. Show the same verse changing.
3. Install a new version, wait for it to finish, use it.
4. Options "Show Bible version" and "Shorten version" (KJV on screen or not).

**Say:** "Pick the version your church reads from. If it isn't installed yet, search for it and add it."

**In this video:** switch version, install a new version, show or hide the version name.

---

## 4. How to compare two Bible translations on screen (2 min)

**Goal:** the viewer can show two versions of the same verse together.

**Show**
1. Open Compare, choose **Translations**, set Translation A and Translation B (for example KJV and NIV).
2. Choose the layout: **Side by side** and **Line by line**. Send each and show the difference on stream.
3. Compare **Passages**: two different passages shown together (for example a prophecy and its fulfilment).
4. Turn Compare off to go back to one version.

**Say:** "Side by side is best on a wide screen. Line by line reads better for longer verses."

**In this video:** compare two translations, choose side by side or line by line, compare two passages, turn it off.

---

## 5. Optional: how to change how verses look (3 min)

**Goal:** the viewer can change the look without opening a design tool. Open with: "Everything so far worked with the default look. If you want your verses to match your church, here is how."

**Show**
1. Start with Quick Settings, which are the simple changes (they update the Dock preview live):
   - Text size, and Fit text to frame
   - Lines per stage (how many lines per screen)
   - Text alignment, font, colours
2. Reference display: full (John 3:16), short, or off; its position; the reference background (solid, outline, pill).
3. Show verse numbers on or off; shorten book names.
4. Only now, and briefly: Change Theme, to pick a ready-made look, with the favorites list. Say "For making your own themes, see the Themes playlist" and stop there.
5. Check the verse looks good in both lower third and full screen.

**Say:** "Change one setting at a time and watch the preview. When it looks right, send it."

**In this video:** size the text, set lines per screen, style the reference, and pick a ready-made look.

---

## 6. How to run Bible verses from the keyboard (1 to 2 min)

**Goal:** the viewer can follow a sermon without using the mouse.

**Show** (with the keys on screen)
- Down arrow: next verse. Up arrow: previous verse.
- Right arrow: next chapter. Left arrow: previous chapter.
- Shift plus Right or Left: next or previous book.
- Enter: send the selected verse to the screen.
- Esc: clear.
- Demonstrate by following a short reading, verse after verse.

**Say:** "Once you know these keys, you can keep up with a pastor who jumps from verse to verse."

**In this video:** the arrow keys for verses, chapters and books, Enter to send, Esc to clear.

**Short answer:** Loghema (the new name for LogosAI) listens to a sermon, detects Bible verses, and also handles worship lyrics and sermon notes. MakeChurchEazy covers the same ground and adds control of OBS Studio itself: it works as a dock inside OBS and creates the scenes and sources for you. MakeChurchEazy also starts cheaper: from $7 USD a month, while Loghema says paid plans start from $14. Loghema offers unlimited transcription on its Pro and Max plans, and more Bible versions.

*Details checked on October 22, 2026 from public websites. Confirm current prices on each site.*

## Why a small church might pick MakeChurchEazy

- **Lower price.** Starts at $7 a month, or ₦2,700 in Nigeria.
- **Built around OBS.** The control panel sits inside OBS and manages the scenes and sources, so a volunteer does not need to learn OBS in depth.
- **More than scripture.** Countdowns, tickers, multi-view and multistreaming are in the same app.
- **Grows with you.** Cloud sync across operators and multi-campus sync are on higher plans.

## Where Loghema is stronger

- **Cloud song search.** It can search worship sites and a cloud song database for lyrics.
- **Unlimited transcription** on Pro and Max, subject to its fair use policy.
- **Voice commands** for hands-free control.
- **Verse timing.** It says it detects verses in about half a second. We have not tested that, so try both on a real sermon.

## Which one should you choose?

- Choose **MakeChurchEazy** if you run a small team or are a new pastor and want the whole service, OBS included, in one affordable app.
- Choose **Loghema** if AI verse detection with unlimited listening is your main need and you already have your OBS setup sorted.

Both have a free plan, so test them on the same sermon.

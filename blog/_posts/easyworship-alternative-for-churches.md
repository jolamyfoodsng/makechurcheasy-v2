## Why churches look for an EasyWorship alternative

- **They stream now.** Many churches that started with in-room screens now stream to YouTube or Facebook. They want lyrics and verses on the stream without extra windows.
- **Cost.** They want to start free and pay only when the team grows.
- **Simpler for volunteers.** One clear control panel beats a long list of settings on a busy Sunday.
- **Sharing across operators.** Cloud sync lets more than one person prepare content.

## How to move your songs from EasyWorship

EasyWorship import is free on every MakeChurchEazy plan. In short:

1. Find your EasyWorship song data. EasyWorship keeps lyrics and song details in database files, called `SongWords.db` and `Songs.db`. Work from a copy so your originals stay safe.
2. Install MakeChurchEazy on the free plan.
3. Import your songs into the MakeChurchEazy song library.
4. Open a few songs and check the slide breaks.
5. Add the songs to a schedule for your next service.

If you get stuck on step 1 or 3, [contact support](/support) and we will help you through it.

You can also import songs from PDF, Word and PowerPoint files, or paste lyrics in. See [Worship songs and hymns](/docs/worship).

## A safe way to switch

Do not switch the week of a big service. Instead:

1. Keep EasyWorship running as normal.
2. Install MakeChurchEazy beside it and import your songs.
3. Run a midweek rehearsal with the volunteers who run Sunday.
4. Use MakeChurchEazy for one service, with EasyWorship ready as a backup.
5. When the team is comfortable, make it your main app.

**Short answer:** Pewbeam and Loghema are AI tools built mainly to listen to a sermon and put Bible verses on screen. MakeChurchEazy also does that (it is called Verse AI), but it is built to run the whole service: Bible, lyrics, media, lower thirds and countdowns, inside OBS Studio or on a projector. For a small church or a one-person media team, MakeChurchEazy has the lowest starting price: $7 USD a month, against $12 for Pewbeam and from $14 for Loghema.

The trade-off, stated plainly: Pewbeam and Loghema offer unlimited sermon transcription on their middle plans. MakeChurchEazy includes a set number of speech-to-scripture hours each month. We explain what that means below.

*Details checked on October 22, 2026 from each company's public website. Prices and features change, so confirm on their sites before you buy.*

## The three tools in one paragraph each

**Pewbeam** is a desktop app that listens to the preacher and shows the matching Bible verse, including when the preacher paraphrases. It also makes slides with AI, writes sermon notes, and sends output over NDI and HDMI. Its website focuses on scripture, slides and themes.

**Loghema** (formerly LogosAI) is a desktop app that listens to the sermon, detects verses, and waits for an operator to approve them. It also handles worship lyrics, imports from EasyWorship and ProPresenter, sermon notes, voice commands and phone control. It outputs over HDMI and NDI.

**MakeChurchEazy** runs inside OBS Studio as a control panel (a dock) and sends Bible verses, worship lyrics, media, lower thirds, tickers and countdowns to your stream. It creates the OBS scenes and sources for you on paid plans. Studio mode sends the same content to a projector or TV with no OBS. Verse AI listens to the sermon and suggests verses for an operator to review.

## What "4 hours a month" means for a small church

Verse AI uses about one credit per minute while it listens. A weekly one-hour sermon uses roughly 4 to 5 hours a month. So Basic (4 hours) covers about four sermons, and Growth (10 hours) covers a weekly sermon plus a midweek service.

If you want to leave transcription running all week with no limit, Pewbeam Plus or Loghema Pro will suit that better. If you listen only during the sermon, MakeChurchEazy's hours are usually enough.

## Which should a small church choose?

**Choose MakeChurchEazy if:**

- You are a one-person or small volunteer media team.
- You are a new pastor starting out and want a low monthly cost.
- You stream with OBS and want Bible, lyrics, media and lower thirds in one place.

## Common questions

### What is the difference between Pewbeam and Loghema?
Both listen to the sermon and show Bible verses. Pewbeam highlights AI slides and themes. Loghema highlights worship lyrics, imports from EasyWorship and ProPresenter, and phone control. Check each site for current details.

### Is Loghema the same as LogosAI?
Yes. Loghema is the new name for LogosAI.

### Which is the cheapest AI church presentation software?
For a paid plan, MakeChurchEazy starts at $7 USD a month, against $12 for Pewbeam and from $14 for Loghema. All three have a free plan.

### Do I need OBS?
No. MakeChurchEazy's Studio mode projects to a screen without OBS. OBS is needed only for the livestream features.

### Can MakeChurchEazy find Bible verses automatically?
Yes. Verse AI listens through a microphone, suggests the verse when it hears a spoken reference, and an operator checks it before it goes on screen.

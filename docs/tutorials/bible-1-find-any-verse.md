# Video 1: How to find any Bible verse fast (about 2 min)

Part of the Bible playlist. Goal: the viewer can open any verse in a few seconds. No themes in this video; use the default look and do not open any theme picker.

**Title:** How to Find Any Bible Verse Fast
**Description:** Open any Bible verse in seconds in MakeChurchEazy: short codes like j316, full references, words from the verse, browsing, recent searches, favorites and history.

## Before recording

- OBS open with a visible preview, Bible tab in the Dock, nothing on screen.
- Clear Bible History so the list is short and clean, then open John 3:16 and Psalm 23:1-3 once so there is something to show.
- Star one passage beforehand (Romans 8:28) so Favorites has an entry.
- Test every search in the script first. If a confirmation box appears when you search by words, keep it in and click through it.
- Mouse cursor large enough to follow. Zoom the Dock to fill the screen.

## Script

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Bible tab, empty search box. Title text: "Find any verse fast" | "In this video you'll learn how to open any Bible verse in about five seconds, without scrolling through books and chapters." |
| 0:08 | Click the search box. Type `j316`. Callout: "j316 = John 3:16" | "Click the search box and type a short code. J, three, sixteen. That's John 3:16. You don't need the full book name, and you don't need the colon." |
| 0:18 | Clear it. Type `ps23`, then `ps 23:1-3`. Callout: "Ranges work too" | "Here's Psalm 23. Add a colon and a range, and you get just those verses." |
| 0:28 | Clear it. Type `john 3:16` in full. Press Enter. Verse opens. | "The full name works too. Use whichever is quicker for you. Press Enter and the verse opens." |
| 0:38 | Clear it. Type `for God so loved the world`. Show the results list. Click the right one. | "Did the pastor quote a verse without saying where it is? Type a few words from it. Pick the right result from the list." |
| 1:00 | Use the Book, Chapter and Verse pickers in turn, to open Romans 8:28. | "If you'd rather browse, choose the book, then the chapter, then the verse." |
| 1:15 | Click the empty search box. The dropdown shows **Recent Searches**. Click one. | "Click the empty search box and your recent searches appear. Click one to open it again." |
| 1:28 | On a verse, click the star next to the reference. Then click the search box again and point to **Favorites**, and "View all". | "Use the star for verses you use every week. They show up under Favorites, right under the search box." |
| 1:42 | Click the history button next to the reference. Show the list, then search it, then sort by newest first. | "Lost a verse from earlier in the service? Open Bible History. Search it, or sort by newest first." |
| 1:58 | Back to the Dock. End card: "Next: put a verse on your stream" | "That's how to find any verse fast. In the next video, I'll show you how to put it on your stream." |

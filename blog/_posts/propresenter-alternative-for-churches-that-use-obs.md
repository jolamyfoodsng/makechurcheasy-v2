**Short answer:** MakeChurchEazy is a church presentation app that works inside OBS Studio. It is a good ProPresenter alternative if your church livestreams with OBS, your operators are mostly volunteers, and you want Bible verses, lyrics, media and lower thirds without building scenes by hand. It has a free plan, paid plans from $7 USD a month, and free ProPresenter import on every plan.

If your current setup works well and your team is happy, you probably do not need to switch. This guide helps you decide.

## Why churches look for a ProPresenter alternative

Common reasons include:

- **Cost.** A small church wants to start free or pay a small monthly amount.
- **OBS first.** The church already streams with OBS and does not want another window to manage on top of it.
- **Volunteer-friendly.** The person running Sunday media may not be technical. Fewer settings means fewer mistakes.
- **Bible tools.** The pastor changes passages on the fly and the team needs to find any verse quickly.

## What MakeChurchEazy does

- **Bible verses in OBS.** Search any reference, show it full screen or as a lower third, compare translations side by side, and use 1,000+ Bible versions in 250+ languages.
- **Worship lyrics.** Keep songs in one library, arrange lyric slides before the service, and pick the verse or chorus as the team sings.
- **Media.** Keep worship backgrounds, sermon clips and announcements beside OBS and send them live.
- **Lower thirds, tickers and countdowns** for speaker names, announcements and the pre-service countdown.
- **Automatic OBS scenes and sources** on paid plans, so you do not build them by hand.
- **Studio mode** for churches that need to project to a screen in the room without OBS.
- **Verse AI** that listens to the sermon and suggests spoken Bible references for your operator to review.

Learn more on the [features page](/features).

## How it works with OBS

MakeChurchEazy connects to OBS Studio through OBS's built-in WebSocket server and adds a control panel (a dock) inside OBS. Your operator picks a verse, a song or a media file in the dock and it goes live. There is no plugin to install for the basics. See the [OBS setup guide](/docs/obs-setup).

## What it costs

- **Free:** $0. Up to 10 songs, lyrics and media, 5 Bible versions, a 30-minute speech-to-scripture trial, and free EasyWorship and ProPresenter import.
- **Basic:** $7 USD a month. Unlimited local songs, lyrics and media, all Bible versions, automatic OBS scenes and sources.
- **Growth:** $12 USD a month. Adds cloud sync across operators, a mobile control app, lower thirds and sermon export.

## Who should choose MakeChurchEazy

It is a good fit if:

- You stream with OBS Studio and want to stay in it.
- Your media team is mostly volunteers.
- You want to start free and grow.
- Bible verses and lyrics are your main needs.
- You want to project in the room as well as stream, from one app.

## Who should stay with ProPresenter

It may be better to stay if:

- Your team already knows ProPresenter well and your Sunday runs smoothly.
- You depend on a specific ProPresenter feature or hardware workflow that you have not confirmed MakeChurchEazy covers.
- You are right in the middle of a busy season, such as Easter or Christmas. Switch in a quiet week.

The best test is simple: try the free plan alongside your current setup for a few services before you decide.

## How to move your songs over

ProPresenter import is free on every MakeChurchEazy plan, so you can bring your song library with you. You can also import songs from PDF, Word and PowerPoint files, or paste lyrics. See [Worship songs and hymns](/docs/worship).

A safe way to switch:

1. Install MakeChurchEazy on the same computer, on the free plan.
2. Import a handful of songs and your next service's passages.
3. Run a midweek rehearsal with the volunteers who will use it.
4. Use it for one livestream while keeping your old setup ready as a backup.
5. Switch fully once your team feels comfortable.

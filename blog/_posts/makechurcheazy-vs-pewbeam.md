**Short answer:** Pewbeam is an AI app that listens to a sermon and shows the matching Bible verse. MakeChurchEazy does that too (Verse AI), and it also runs the rest of your service: lyrics, media, lower thirds and countdowns, inside OBS Studio or on a projector. MakeChurchEazy costs less to start ($7 USD a month for Basic, against $12 for Pewbeam Plus), and it is built for small teams. Pewbeam offers unlimited transcription on its paid plans, which MakeChurchEazy does not.

## Why a small church might pick MakeChurchEazy

- **Lower cost.** Starting paid plan is $7 a month. In Nigeria it is ₦2,700.
- **One app for Sunday.** Bible, lyrics, media and lower thirds sit in one control panel, so a single volunteer can run them.
- **No OBS building work.** On paid plans, MakeChurchEazy creates and updates the OBS scenes and sources for you.
- **Room and stream.** Studio mode puts the same content on a projector or TV when you are not streaming.
- **Easy to grow.** Start free, then add cloud sync, phone control and more as the team grows.

## Where Pewbeam is stronger

- **Unlimited transcription** on Plus and Core. With MakeChurchEazy, Basic includes 4 hours a month, which covers about four one-hour sermons.
- **AI slides.** Pewbeam can generate slides with AI.
- **Paraphrase detection** is the centre of its product. If that is your one big need, try Pewbeam's free minutes first.

## Which one should you choose?

- Choose **MakeChurchEazy** if you are a small team or a new pastor who wants the whole service in one affordable app.
- Choose **Pewbeam** if you mainly want AI verse detection with no time limit and already have your lyrics, media and OBS setup sorted.

Both have a free plan, so try them on the same sermon.

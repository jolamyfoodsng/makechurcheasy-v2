# Bible playlist: scripts for all 20 videos

Simple words, slow pace, one idea at a time. Each video has a timed table (what to show, what to say) and a block to paste into a text-to-voice tool.

## How to use these

- **Voice speed:** about 85%. `...` is a short pause.
- **Start times** are a guide for a slow voice (about 2 words a second). Record the screen first, then fit the voice to it, not the other way round. If a line is longer than the action, hold the screen a little longer.
- **Zoom in** whenever the table says so, then zoom back out. Keep the pointer slow and let it rest 2 seconds on each thing before you click.
- **Same sample verses everywhere:** John 3:16, Psalm 23, Romans 8, Hebrews 4:15.
- **Check every label on screen** before you say it. Some button names come from the app's text and may read a little different.
- **Order:** go from 1 to 20. Each video ends by naming the next one.

## Before you record (things to check)

- **Enter on a typed search** puts the verse on the screen at once (tested). **Enter on a selected verse** may only get it ready without showing it. Use the **Down arrow** or a **click** to show a verse.
- **Videos 8 to 10, 15 and 19** need a paid plan or trial for the full demo. Videos 8, 15 and 19 mention the Free plan limits.
- **Video 8:** you need at least 2 versions installed to show Compare (video 9).
- **Command palette:** not used. It does nothing in this build.
- **Output button (video 19):** it is hidden on the Free plan.
